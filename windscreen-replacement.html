Repair or replace

Does it need
replacing?

Not every damaged windscreen has to come out. These are the things that usually decide it. Send Frank a photo and he will tell you which way it goes for your car.

Size and spread

A small, fresh chip can often be fixed with a stone chip repair. Once it has run into a crack, or several chips sit close together, a new windscreen is usually the safer option.

Where it sits

Damage in the driver’s line of sight, or close to the edge of the glass, is harder to repair well. It affects what you see and how the screen holds together.

How deep it goes

A windscreen is laminated: two layers of glass with a plastic layer between them. Repairs only work on the outer layer. If the damage goes deeper, the screen needs replacing.
