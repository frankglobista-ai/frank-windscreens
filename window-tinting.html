Queensland rules

How dark
can you go?

Queensland sets a minimum amount of light that tinted windows must let through. For cars, utes, 4WDs and other light vehicles:

Front side windows

The driver and front passenger windows must let through at least 35% of light (T35).

Behind the driver

Windows behind the driver’s seat can go as dark as 20% (T20), as long as the vehicle has a mirror on each side.

Factory-tinted glass counts

If the glass is already tinted, the film and the glass together still have to meet the limit.

Source: Queensland Government vehicle standards for light vehicles. Frank will go through what suits your vehicle before anything is fitted.

How dark can my front windows be in Queensland?

The driver and front passenger windows must let through at least 35% of light. Behind the driver the limit is 20%, as long as the vehicle has a mirror on each side.

Can you tint over factory-tinted glass?

The film and the glass together must still meet Queensland’s limit, so ask Frank what your vehicle can take.
