Which glass

What you
might need

Door glass

The window that winds up and down in the door. When it breaks it usually shatters into small pieces, so the door is cleared out before the new pane goes in.

Quarter glass

The small fixed pane beside the door or at the rear pillar.

Rear windscreen

The back window, which often has demister lines built into the glass.

Until you get here

If a window
has just broken

  • Cover the opening to keep the rain out, especially in the wet season
  • Take valuables out of the car
  • Watch for loose glass on the seats and in the door
